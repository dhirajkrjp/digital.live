Privacy Policy

This policy explains how D25 Assistant collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you connect a Google account.

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Overview

D25 Assistant is a personal AI workspace agent operated by Dhiraj Kumar. It connects with Google Workspace to help a signed-in user search, summarize, draft, and, only with approval, update Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and Google Tasks.

By using D25 Assistant and completing Google sign-in, you authorize D25 to access only the Google permissions you approve. We do not obtain or request your Google password.

2. Data we access

Depending on the permissions you grant, D25 Assistant may access:

  • Google account information: basic profile details such as name and email address used to identify your account.
  • Gmail: messages, threads, metadata, drafts, labels, and related content needed to read, search, draft, send, or archive content at your request.
  • Google Calendar: calendar events and availability needed to read, create, update, or cancel events at your request.
  • Google Sheets: spreadsheet names, sheet ranges, and cell values needed to read or update content at your request.
  • Google Tasks: task lists and task content needed to create, complete, update, or manage tasks at your request.
  • Application data: OAuth session information, chat prompts and responses, activity history, undo snapshots, saved preferences, and approval decisions.

D25 does not intentionally request unrelated Google account data. Access is limited to the permissions shown on the Google consent screen.

3. How we use data

  • Authenticate you and keep your workspace session associated with your account.
  • Provide chat, search, summarization, planning, and action features across connected Google services.
  • Execute write operations such as sending email, creating calendar events, editing spreadsheets, or updating tasks only after the corresponding in-app approval.
  • Maintain account-isolated session history and provide undo or audit context for actions where supported.
  • Diagnose application errors, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

We do not sell your personal information or Google user data. We do not use Google user data to serve advertising.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and account-associated session data are intended to be stored separately by signed-in account. D25 uses access controls and account-isolated application storage to limit access to your data.

Google OAuth communication should use HTTPS and OAuth 2.0. No internet-based storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your Google account credentials and device protected.

5. Sharing and third parties

D25 does not sell or rent Google user data. Data may be processed by the service providers necessary to operate D25, including Google APIs and infrastructure or AI-model providers used to process authorized prompts and workspace context.

Data may be disclosed if required to comply with law, protect users or the public, or enforce applicable terms. D25 does not permit third parties to use Google user data for unrelated advertising or independent profiling.

6. Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials and account-associated chat or activity data are retained while needed to provide D25 features or until you revoke access or request deletion.

To request deletion of D25 data associated with your account, email dhiraj7kr@gmail.com from the Google account you used. You may also revoke D25 access at any time in Google Account Permissions.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You decide whether to sign in and which Google permissions to grant.
  • You can revoke access from your Google account at any time.
  • Read operations may occur automatically after authorization, while write operations require visible approval in the interface.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of application data associated with your account.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

D25 Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the D25 Assistant features described in this policy or for security and fraud prevention. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold or shared for unrelated purposes.
